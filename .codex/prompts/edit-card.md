Review this card before it ships: $ARGUMENTS

Read `docs/card-style-guide.md`, `docs/cards/tang.md`, and the research brief at `docs/cards/_research/<slug>.md` so you can check the prose against the evidence it came from.

You have authority to block. Say plainly when a card is not ready. Do not pad the report with praise.

**1. Sourcing integrity, first and hardest.** An earlier Tang draft tracked Mark Edward Lewis, *China's Cosmopolitan Empire*, at the level of phrasing, and the whole corpus was AI-generated before the rewrite began. Take distinctive sentences and arguments and search for them. A card that reads like one book walked through in order is a failure even where nothing is verbatim.

**2. Accuracy.** Verify every date, figure, reign length and name against the brief, and against a source where the brief is thin. Check that contested material is marked contested and that traditional or legendary attributions are not presented as established fact. Treat any suspiciously round or confident number as suspect.

**3. Voice.** Reject on sight: em dashes; "not X, but Y" in any variant; "-ing" tails (", highlighting", ", reflecting", ", ensuring"); "serves as", "stands as", "represents", "boasts", "features" where "is" or "has" belongs; storytelling drift (scene-setting openings, second-person address, dramatic one-liners, rhetorical questions); promotional adjectives; rule-of-three flourishes; American spelling. Grep for the mechanical ones, read for the rest.

**4. Structure.** Lead paragraph states what the thing is in 60 to 90 words with no hook. Section headings short and substantive, not "Background" or "Legacy". A closing section on memory and historiography. Total word count should match the card's band: 900 to 1500 for a dynasty or period, 500 to 800 for everything else. Flag it rather than silently enforcing it if the actual scope clearly justifies going outside the band.

**5. Chinese terms.** Simplified throughout, no traditional mixed in. Tone marks present and correct; verify them, since wrong tones are the most common silent error. First mention only, personal names capitalised, institutional terms lower case.

**6. Argument.** Does the card make a claim, or narrate in date order? State the argument back in one sentence. If you cannot, that is the finding.

Fix trivial mechanical violations directly. Report anything substantive and leave it. Give a verdict: ship, ship with fixes, or rewrite.
