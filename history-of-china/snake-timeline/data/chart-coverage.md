# Reference chart coverage

The mapping preserves the chart’s subjects while checking their terminology and chronology. Existing analytical cards are reused where they already explain the subject. Twenty-two missing topics receive new cards; eight existing introductions explicitly identify broader chart themes.

The chart’s 475 BCE Warring States boundary and the catalogue’s 481 BCE boundary are both acknowledged; no dates were silently changed. “Five Barbarians” is identified as inherited derogatory terminology, not adopted as a neutral classification. Niu–Li factional coherence is contested. Yu’s flood-control tradition is not presented as a securely dated historical event.
